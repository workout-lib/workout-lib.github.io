{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
If it is your first time doing this exercise, set the counterweight to about two-thirds of your body weight or more. The higher the weight you set, the easier it will be. Use this setting while you learn the proper form. Make sure the pin is securely locked into the weight stack before stepping on.

### Step 2
Place your knees firmly on the padded platform.

### Step 3
Keep your body in a straight line from your head down to your knees. Tighten your core muscles to stay stable throughout the exercise.

### Step 4
Move smoothly up and down, feeling a gentle stretch in your chest at the bottom and squeezing your chest and triceps at the top.

### Step 5
Keep your shoulders relaxed and pulled down away from your ears. Grip the bars with your hands positioned directly under your shoulders.

### Step 6
Bend your elbows and let them point straight back as you lower your body down. Stop when your elbows bend to about a 90-degree angle.

### Step 7
Exhale as you press through your hands to push yourself back up.

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
Lower the machine's counterweight as you get stronger. On this machine, choosing less weight makes the exercise harder because you are lifting more of your own body weight. 
{{< /callout >}}
