{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Set the bench to a 30–45 degree incline. Lie back on the bench, plant your feet flat on the floor wider than shoulder-width, and keep your hips, upper back, and head firmly against the pad.

### Step 2
Grip the barbell with an overhand grip slightly wider than shoulder-width.

### Step 3
Take a deep breath and slowly lower the barbell down to your upper chest.

### Step 4
Exhale and press the barbell straight back up to the starting position.


{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Master proper form before adding heavier weights.

• Do not use momentum or bounce the bar off your chest—keep the movement slow and controlled.

• Keep your chest lifted and your shoulder blades pulled down and back.

• Exhale as you push the barbell up.

• Keep your feet planted firmly and wide on the floor for maximum stability.
{{< /callout >}}
