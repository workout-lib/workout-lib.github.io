{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Wrap the resistance band across your upper back and hold the ends securely under the palms of your hands.

### Step 2
Get into a push-up position on your hands and toes, placing your hands slightly wider than shoulder-width, aligned with your upper chest.

### Step 3
Straighten your body so your head, shoulders, hips, and heels form a single straight line.

### Step 4
Inhale and lower yourself down until your chest is just above the floor.

### Step 5
Exhale and push yourself back up with control, squeezing your chest against the resistance of the band.

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Do not relax at the bottom—keep constant tension in your muscles throughout the entire movement.

• Keep your body completely straight; do not let your lower back sag or hunch your shoulders.

• Perform every rep smoothly and with control, focusing on the work in your chest.
{{< /callout >}}
