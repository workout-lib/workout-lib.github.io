{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Adjust the seat height so the handles align with your mid-chest or slightly below shoulder level. Sit up tall with your back and head firmly against the backrest. Place your feet flat on the floor directly under your knees (knees bent at a 90-degree angle).

### Step 2
Grip the handles with an overhand grip (palms facing forward) at shoulder-width or slightly wider. This is your starting position.

### Step 3
Exhale and press the handles forward away from your chest.

### Step 4
Pause briefly at full extension (without locking your elbows), then smoothly lower the handles back to the starting position.

### Step 5
As soon as the handles return close to your chest, immediately press forward again without pausing at the bottom.

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• When using heavier weights, push your feet firmly into the floor and keep your back and hips pressed against the seat.

• At the starting position, do not let your elbows pull too far back behind your body.

• When returning the handles to your chest, keep your elbows pointed out to the sides rather than driving them far behind your back to protect your shoulder joints.
{{< /callout >}}
