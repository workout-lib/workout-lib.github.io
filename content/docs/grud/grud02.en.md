{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Stand straight with your feet shoulder-width apart. Keep your back flat and your head in a natural, neutral position.

### Step 2
Squeeze a weight plate between the palms of your hands and hold it straight out in front of you at chest height.

### Step 3
Inhale and slowly bring the plate in toward your chest, letting your elbows move out to the sides.

### Step 4
Touch the plate lightly to your chest, then exhale and slowly press it back out to the starting position. 

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
﻿﻿• Do not use momentum—perform the exercise slowly and with full control.
  
• Keep the plate moving parallel to the floor with your elbows flared out to the sides.

• Maintain a steady breathing rhythm throughout the movement.
{{< /callout >}}
