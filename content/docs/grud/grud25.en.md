{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Lie perpendicular across a flat bench with only your upper back and shoulders supported on the pad. Keep your hips suspended, knees bent, and feet planted flat on the floor for support (forming a T-shape with the bench). Hold the dumbbell over your chest with both hands pressing up against the underside of the top plate, keeping a slight bend in your elbows.

### Step 2
Take a deep breath and slowly lower the dumbbell back over your head in an arc, keeping your arms nearly straight. To stay balanced and get a deeper stretch, lower your hips slightly as the dumbbell reaches the bottom.

### Step 3
Exhale and pull the dumbbell back up in an arc over your chest, engaging your chest and lats.


{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Do not use momentum—keep the movement slow and controlled through the full range of motion. Keep your neck relaxed and aligned with your spine.

• Work through as deep a stretch as your shoulder flexibility comfortably allows.

• Maintain steady breathing throughout the exercise.
{{< /callout >}}
