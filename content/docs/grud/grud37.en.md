{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Adjust the seat height so the handles are positioned slightly below shoulder level.

### Step 2
Take the starting position: Sit on the machine, grab the handles, and bring them together in front of your chest. Keep a slight arch in your lower back and press your shoulder blades firmly against the backrest.

### Step 3
Keep one arm held steady in front of you, and on an inhale, slowly open the working arm out to the side.

### Step 4
Exhale and bring the working arm back to the center to meet your other hand.

### Step 5
Complete the desired number of reps, then switch and perform the exercise with the other arm.

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Maintain a slow, controlled tempo and avoid using momentum.

• Keep your back against the backrest with your shoulder blades pulled together and elbows pointed out to the sides.

• As you bring your arm forward, squeeze your chest muscles firmly; as you open your arm back, keep a soft bend in your elbow to feel a safe stretch in your chest.

• Maintain a steady breathing rhythm throughout the exercise.
{{< /callout >}}
