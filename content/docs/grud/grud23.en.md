{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Place the push-up handles slightly wider than shoulder-width. Grip the handles and get into a push-up position on your toes with your arms extended and your body forming a straight line.

### Step 2
Slowly lower your body as deep as comfortably possible, letting your elbows flare slightly out to the sides.

### Step 3
Push firmly through your hands to press yourself back up, squeezing your chest muscles at the top.


{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Keep your back flat throughout the entire movement—do not let your hips sag or arch upward.

• Focus on feeling your chest muscles stretch on the way down and contract on the way up.

• Lower yourself down slowly (take 2–3 times longer to descend than to push back up).
{{< /callout >}}
