{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
If the machine allows, adjust the handle height so they rest slightly below chest level.

### Step 2
Set the desired weight and lie back on the bench so the handles align with your lower chest. Keep a slight arch in your lower back, pull your shoulders down, and squeeze your shoulder blades firmly against the bench.

### Step 3
Grip the handles. Exhale, tighten your chest muscles, press your shoulder blades into the bench, and press the handles upward until your arms are extended. Hold for one second at the top, squeezing your chest.

### Step 4
Inhale and slowly lower the handles back down to the starting position.


{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Work through a full range of motion.

• Do not let the weights or handles rest on the safety stoppers at the bottom—keep the tension in your chest muscles the entire time.

• Maintain a steady breathing rhythm.
{{< /callout >}}
