{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Adjust the seat height so the handles are level with your lower chest. Sit sideways on the seat with your non-working shoulder resting firmly against the backrest. Sit up tall, pull your shoulders down, and squeeze your shoulder blades together. Place your feet flat on the floor directly under your knees.

### Step 2
Grip the handle with one hand using an overhand grip, keeping your wrist straight and aligned with your forearm.

### Step 3
Exhale and press the handle forward away from your chest.

### Step 4
Pause briefly at the end of the press, then inhale and smoothly return the handle to the starting position.

### Step 5
Once the handle gets close to your chest, immediately begin the next rep without stopping.

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Do not let your shoulder or back come off the backrest.

• Do not lock out your elbow at the end of the push—keep a slight bend to protect your joint.

• Maintain steady breathing throughout the exercise.
{{< /callout >}}
