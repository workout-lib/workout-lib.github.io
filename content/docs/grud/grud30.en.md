{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Sit on an exercise mat holding a dumbbell in each hand resting on your thighs. Carefully roll back onto the floor, bringing the weights with you.

### Step 2
Press the dumbbells straight up above your chest, keeping a soft, fixed bend in your elbows.

### Step 3
Bend your knees and place your feet flat on the floor shoulder-width apart.

### Step 4
Inhale and lower your arms out to the sides in a wide arc until the backs of your upper arms gently touch the floor.

### Step 5
Do not let your muscles relax at the bottom of the movement.

### Step 6
Exhale and bring the dumbbells back together over your chest, imagining you are hugging a wide barrel.

### Step 7
Hold for a brief second at the top, squeezing your chest muscles firmly.

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Focus on actively engaging and feeling your chest muscles throughout the movement.

• Maintain a steady breathing rhythm.

• Avoid using momentum—keep every repetition slow and under control.
{{< /callout >}}
