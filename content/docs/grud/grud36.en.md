{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Sit on the machine and adjust the seat height so the handles align comfortably with your mid-chest.

### Step 2
Grip the handles with both hands. This is your starting position.

### Step 3
Exhale and bring the handles together all the way in front of your chest. Feel your chest muscles contract and hold the squeeze for one second.

### Step 4
Inhale and slowly return to the starting position. Repeat for the desired number of reps.

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Keep your upper back and shoulders pressed firmly against the backrest with your shoulder blades pulled together throughout the exercise.

• Keep your elbows pointed out to the sides rather than down; maintain a soft bend in your elbows when opening your arms, and extend them slightly as you bring your hands together to maximize chest contraction.

• Adjusting the seat height allows you to target different areas of the chest.

• Open your arms only until your elbows are in line with your shoulders to get a safe and effective stretch.
{{< /callout >}}
