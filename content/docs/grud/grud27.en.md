{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Lie back on a flat bench and plant your feet flat on the floor shoulder-width apart for stability.

### Step 2
Hold the dumbbells directly above your chest with your palms facing each other. Keep a slight, fixed bend in your elbows throughout the entire movement.

### Step 3
Inhale and slowly lower your arms out to the sides in a wide arc until your elbows are roughly level with your chest and shoulders.

### Step 4
Exhale, squeeze your chest muscles, and bring the dumbbells back together over your chest along the same wide arc path.

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Master proper form with lighter weights before trying to lift heavier.

• Do not use momentum—perform the exercise slowly and with control.

• Never lock your elbows out straight or lower the dumbbells too far down to prevent shoulder strain. Keep a constant, soft bend in your elbows.

• Focus on squeezing your chest muscles to bring the weights together.

• Exhale as you bring the dumbbells together over your chest.
{{< /callout >}}
