{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Stand up straight with your feet shoulder-width apart. Keep your back flat and your head in a natural, neutral position.

### Step 2
Grip the end of the barbell sleeve with both hands and hold it straight out in front of you at chest height.

### Step 3
Inhale and slowly bring the bar toward your chest, letting your elbows move out to the sides.

### Step 4
Touch the end of the bar lightly to your chest, then exhale and slowly press it back out to the starting position.

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Do not use momentum—perform the exercise slowly and with full control through the entire range of motion.

• Maintain a steady breathing rhythm throughout the movement.
{{< /callout >}}
