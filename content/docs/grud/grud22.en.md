{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Get into a push-up position with your hands placed shoulder-width apart, aligned with your upper chest.

### Step 2
Keep your body in a straight line and lower yourself toward the floor.

### Step 3
Let your elbows point slightly back and outward as you lower down.

### Step 4
From the bottom position, push firmly through your hands to press your body back up to full arm extension.

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Move slowly and with control, feeling your chest and arm muscles work.

• Keep your head, shoulders, hips, and feet in a straight line throughout the entire set.

• Do not bounce or use momentum—lift your body using muscle strength alone.
{{< /callout >}}
