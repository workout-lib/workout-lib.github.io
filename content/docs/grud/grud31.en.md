{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Sit on the machine and adjust the seat height so your upper arms form roughly an 80–90 degree angle with your torso (elbows aligned with your mid-chest). Squeeze your shoulder blades together against the backrest.

### Step 2
Exhale and bring the handles (or arm pads) together in front of your chest, feeling a strong contraction in your chest muscles.

### Step 3
Inhale and slowly return your arms to the starting position with full control.

### Step 4
Repeat for the desired number of reps.


{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Perform the movement with control, avoiding any sudden jerks or momentum.

• Maintain steady breathing, exhaling as you bring the handles together.

• Keep your shoulder blades pulled back against the pad throughout the entire set.

• Keep constant tension in your chest—do not let the weight stack touch down or relax your muscles at the stretched position.
{{< /callout >}}
