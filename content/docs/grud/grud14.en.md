{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Lie back on a flat bench with your hips pressed firmly against the pad. Plant your feet flat on the floor so you feel completely stable.

### Step 2
Grip the barbell with an overhand grip slightly wider than shoulder-width (a wider grip puts more focus on the chest, while a narrower grip works the triceps more).

### Step 3
Inhale and slowly lower the bar down to your mid-chest in a smooth, controlled motion.

### Step 4
Exhale and press the bar back up to the starting position.


{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Lower the bar to the middle of your chest—never drop it toward your neck, shoulders, face, or stomach, as this can injure your shoulders.

• Keep your hips firmly on the bench; lifting your hips off the pad compromises your stability.

• Always keep your feet planted flat on the floor for solid balance and proper body mechanics.

• Squeeze your chest muscles at the top. Working through a full, controlled range of motion engages more muscle fibers and gives the best results.

• Always use a full, secure grip with your thumbs wrapped around the bar—never use a thumbless ("suicide") grip.
{{< /callout >}}
