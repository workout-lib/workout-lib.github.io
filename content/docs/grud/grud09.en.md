### Step 1
Starting position: Lie back on a flat bench, holding the dumbbells at chest level with your elbows pointed out to the sides and palms facing forward.

### Step 2
Take a deep breath and press the dumbbells straight up until your arms are fully extended. Exhale at the top.

### Step 3
Smoothly lower the dumbbells back down to the starting position.

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Master proper form before increasing the weight.

• Do not use momentum—keep the movement slow and controlled.

• Focus on pushing the weight with your chest muscles rather than just your arms. Using a lighter weight makes it easier to feel this connection. A common beginner mistake is pressing with the arms instead of engaging the chest.
{{< /callout >}}
