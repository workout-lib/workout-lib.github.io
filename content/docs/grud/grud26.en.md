{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Sit on the end of the bench and hold the dumbbell with both hands resting under the top weight plate. Inhale, brace your core, and carefully lie back flat along the bench. Hold the dumbbell straight up over your chest with a slight bend in your elbows.

### Step 2
Keep your upper back and hips pressed firmly against the bench with your feet planted flat on the floor. Engaging your legs and core helps keep your entire body stable throughout the movement.

### Step 3
Inhale and slowly lower the dumbbell back and down in a wide arc behind your head until you feel a good stretch in your chest and lats. Exhale and pull the dumbbell smoothly back over your chest, squeezing your chest muscles.

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Pullovers focus heavily on stretching and expanding the chest and rib cage under control.

• Focus on feeling the stretch in your chest, lats, and the sides of your torso to ensure safe, proper form.

• Work through a full range of motion, lowering the weight only as deep as your shoulders comfortably allow.

• Always warm up your shoulders thoroughly before doing pullovers, as this exercise places significant demand on the shoulder joints.
{{< /callout >}}
