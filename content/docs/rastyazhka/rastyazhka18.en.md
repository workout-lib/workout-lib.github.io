{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Stand up straight. Take a wide step out to the side with one leg.

### Step 2
Smoothly bend that knee and lower into a side lunge while keeping your other leg completely straight. Make sure to keep the foot of the bent leg flat on the floor.

### Step 3
Hold this position for 15–20 seconds, feeling the stretch along the inner thigh of your straight leg, then slowly push back up to the starting position.

### Step 4
Complete the desired number of reps, then repeat the stretch on the other side.

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Never push the stretch to the point of sharp pain.

• Try to keep your inner thigh muscles relaxed during the stretch.

• Breathe naturally and easily—do not hold your breath.
{{< /callout >}}
