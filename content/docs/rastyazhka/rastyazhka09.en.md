### Step 1
Starting position: Get onto all fours on your hands and knees. Place your wrists directly under your shoulders and your knees directly under your hips (knees bent at a 90-degree angle). Keep your feet relaxed with toes pointing straight back.

### Step 2
Cow Pose: Inhale and slowly drop your belly toward the floor, arching your back down. Open your chest and gently lift your chin and gaze up toward the ceiling (be gentle with your neck).

### Step 3
Cat Pose: Exhale and round your spine upward toward the ceiling like a stretching cat. Press firmly into the floor with your hands, tuck your tailbone in, and draw your chin toward your chest to stretch your back and neck.

### Step 4
Flow smoothly between the two poses: inhale into Cow Pose, then exhale into Cat Pose for several breath cycles.

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Focus on moving through your entire spine, especially opening up the chest and mid-back. Keep your arms straight and shoulders active without collapsing into them.

• Match your movement to your breath: take a full, deep inhale as you arch down, and a complete exhale as you round up.
{{< /callout >}}
