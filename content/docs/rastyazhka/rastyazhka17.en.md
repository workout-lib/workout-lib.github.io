{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Kneel on the floor and sit back on your heels. Place your right forearm flat on the floor in front of you for support, and place your left hand behind your head.

### Step 2
Rotate your upper body and chest open to the left, feeling a stretch through your mid-to-upper back and obliques.

### Step 3
Hold this position for the recommended amount of time (usually 15–30 seconds).

### Step 4
Return to the center and repeat the stretch on the other side.

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Try to keep the target muscles relaxed during the stretch.

• Breathe slowly and steadily.

• Do not force the rotation—stretch only within a comfortable, pain-free range.
{{< /callout >}}
