{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Sit comfortably on the floor in a cross-legged position.

### Step 2
Raise your left arm overhead and lean your torso to the right, placing your right forearm (or hand) on the floor for support.

### Step 3
Lean into the side bend as far as comfortable, feeling the stretch along the side of your body, your obliques, and your back muscles.

### Step 4
Hold this position for the recommended amount of time (usually 15–30 seconds).

### Step 5
Return to the center and repeat the stretch on the other side.

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Try to keep the target muscles relaxed during the stretch.

• Breathe slowly and steadily.

• Do not force the stretch—stretch only until you feel comfortable tension, never sharp pain.
{{< /callout >}}
