{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Stand up straight and place your hands on your hips or lower back for support.

### Step 2
Gently arch your back backward, opening your chest and letting your head tilt naturally back.

### Step 3
Press your hands gently into your lower back to help maintain stability and balance.

### Step 4
Arch back comfortably as far as feels good, opening up your chest and feeling a stretch across your abs and torso.

### Step 5
Hold this position for the recommended amount of time (usually 15–30 seconds).

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Try to keep the target muscles relaxed during the stretch.

• Breathe slowly and steadily.

• Do not force the stretch—stretch only until you feel comfortable tension, never sharp pain.
{{< /callout >}}
