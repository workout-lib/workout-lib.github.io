{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Sit on the floor and spread your legs as wide as comfortably possible, keeping your toes pointing straight up toward the ceiling.

### Step 2
Hinge forward from your hips with a flat back and place your hands on the floor in front of you.

### Step 3
Walk your hands forward until you feel a good stretch along your inner thighs and hamstrings.

### Step 4
Hold this position for the recommended amount of time (usually 15–30 seconds).

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Try to keep the target muscles relaxed during the stretch.

• Breathe slowly and steadily.

• Do not force the stretch—stretch only until you feel comfortable tension, never sharp pain.
{{< /callout >}}
