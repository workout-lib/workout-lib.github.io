{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Sit on a workout mat, bend your knees, and let them fall open to the sides.

### Step 2
Bring the soles of your feet together and use your hands to pull your heels as close to your groin as comfortably possible. Try to let your knees sink toward the floor (it is completely fine if they stay high off the ground at first—your flexibility will improve over time).

### Step 3
Sit up tall with a straight back and open chest. Lengthen your spine upward and tilt your chin slightly down.

### Step 4
Gently lift your knees up off the floor and bring them closer together while keeping the soles of your feet pressed together (you can guide them with your hands). Hold for a few seconds.

### Step 5
Lower your knees back down toward the floor, gently resting your hands on your thighs to deepen the stretch if comfortable. Hold the stretch again. Repeat for the desired number of reps or hold the pose for the recommended time.

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Breathe slowly and steadily—never hold your breath.

• Move smoothly and gently; avoid bouncing or forcing the stretch.
{{< /callout >}}
