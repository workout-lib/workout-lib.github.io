{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Get down onto the floor.

### Step 2
Bend your front leg in front of you and lower your hip toward the floor.

### Step 3
Extend your other leg straight behind you, resting your back knee and the top of your foot on the floor (do not let your knee hover or twist to the side).

### Step 4
Place your hands on the floor in front of you for support, keep a slight natural arch in your lower back, and lift your chin slightly.

### Step 5
Roll your shoulders back and open up your chest.

### Step 6
Feel the stretch in the glute of your front leg and along the hip and thigh of your back leg.

### Step 7
Hold this position for the recommended amount of time (usually 15–30 seconds).

### Step 8
Switch sides and repeat the stretch on the other leg.

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
• Try to keep the target muscles relaxed during the stretch.

• Maintain slow, steady breathing throughout the hold.
{{< /callout >}}
