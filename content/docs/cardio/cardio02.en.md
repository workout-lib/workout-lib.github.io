{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Step onto the treadmill and select your desired workout program.

### Step 2
The settings on most treadmills can be adjusted manually. You will usually need to enter your age and weight to estimate the number of calories burned during your workout.

### Step 3
The treadmill incline can be adjusted manually at any time.

### Step 4
Hold the treadmill's heart rate sensor handles to view your heart rate on the display and choose the appropriate workout intensity.

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
⠀Before starting your workout, familiarize yourself with the treadmill and its control panel. Make sure you know how to adjust the speed and incline and how to stop the treadmill.
⠀Maintain proper posture throughout your workout: keep your shoulders back, engage your abdominal muscles, and look straight ahead rather than down.
⠀Use your natural stride length.
⠀Do not lean to the sides or backward while walking.
⠀For best results, exercise regularly at approximately the same time and keep your workouts consistent in duration.
{{< /callout >}}
