### Step 1
Sit on the rowing machine. Make sure your feet are securely and comfortably strapped into the footrests. Choose your workout program, then lean forward from your hips.

### Step 2
There are three main phases to each rowing stroke:
• First (The Catch): Lean forward with a straight back. Your knees are bent close to your chest, and your arms are reached forward holding the handle.
• Second (The Drive): Push firmly with your legs to straighten them, then pull the handle smoothly toward your belly while squeezing your shoulder blades together.
• Third (The Recovery): Extend your arms back forward, bend your knees, and slide your body forward to get ready for the next stroke.
