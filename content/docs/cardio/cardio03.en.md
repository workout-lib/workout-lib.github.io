{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Adjust the seat height so that when you sit down, your leg on the lowest pedal is almost straight (keep just a slight bend in your knee).

### Step 2
Sit down on the machine and grab the handles.

### Step 3
Start pedaling and move the handles back and forth in rhythm with your legs.


{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
Keep your breathing smooth and steady, breathing through your nose.
Watch your heart rate during your workout. If it gets too high, slow down.
{{< /callout >}}
