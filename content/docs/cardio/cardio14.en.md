### Step 1
Step onto the elliptical machine and select your workout program. Most machines allow you to customize the settings manually—usually by entering your age and weight to estimate calories burned. You can change the resistance level at any time during your workout.

### Step 2
Grip the handles (holding the pulse sensors) so your heart rate appears on the screen, and adjust your workout intensity as needed.
