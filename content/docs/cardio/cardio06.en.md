{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Stand next to your bike, facing the handlebars. Swing your leg over the saddle and place your foot on the ground on the other side.

### Step 2
Rotate the pedals so your front pedal is raised up at roughly a 45-degree angle.

### Step 3
Step down on the raised pedal with your lead foot like you are stepping onto a stair. The bike will start rolling forward.

### Step 4
Sit onto the saddle. As the second pedal comes up to the top, place your other foot on it.

### Step 5
Keep pedaling smoothly—moving forward makes it much easier to keep your balance.

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
⠀• Adjust the saddle height to fit you before riding.
⠀• When sitting comfortably, you should be able to rest your heel on the lowest pedal with your leg straight (without locking your knee or stretching).
⠀• Keep your breathing steady and watch your heart rate.
⠀• Brake smoothly and gradually; avoid squeezing the brakes too hard so the wheels don't lock up.
⠀• Always put on a helmet before you start riding.
⠀• Ride smoothly and avoid sudden, jerky turns—they make it easier to lose balance.
⠀• Look ahead at the road, not down at your front wheel.
{{< /callout >}}
