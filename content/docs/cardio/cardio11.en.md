{{% steps %}}

### Step 1
Adjust the seat height to fit your body. When sitting comfortably, you should be able to rest your heels on the lowest pedals with your legs straight without locking your knees.

### Step 2
Sit on the bike and grip the handlebars.

### Step 3
Start pedaling.

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
⠀Keep an eye on your breathing and heart rate.
{{< /callout >}}
